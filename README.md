# Power BI Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI sales analytics dashboard** designed to provide a clear view of overall sales performance, profitability, product contribution, and growth trends.

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard brings key sales metrics together into a single interactive report, helping users quickly understand business performance and identify important trends.

### Key Metrics

* **Total Sales**
* **Total Profit**
* **Total Quantity**
* **Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth**

### Visual Analysis

* **Monthly Sales Trend** – tracks sales performance over time.
* **Rolling 12-Month Sales** – provides a smoother view of long-term sales performance.
* **Product Performance** – compares sales across individual products.
* **Profitability Analysis** – provides profit information through interactive tooltips.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI**
* **DAX**
* **Data Modelling**
* **Data Visualization**
* **Business Intelligence**

## 📐 Key DAX Concepts

The dashboard uses measures for:

* Total Sales
* Total Quantity
* Total Profit
* YoY Growth %
* Rolling 12-Month Sales

These measures demonstrate the use of DAX for creating dynamic business metrics and time-based analysis.

## 📈 Business Questions Answered

The dashboard helps answer questions such as:

1. What are the overall sales and profit levels?
2. How are sales changing over time?
3. Is the business experiencing year-over-year growth?
4. Which products contribute most to sales?
5. How does product performance vary across the portfolio?
6. What does the longer-term rolling sales trend look like?

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project was to transform sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that allows users to move from **high-level KPIs to detailed product-level insights**.
